P/01 Sparse weather inferenceDaily rainfall between gauges in India, scored the way crop insurance would use it. +13.1% / +11.4% vs krigingtwo locked blind years 2026
research

Crop-insurance payouts in India depend on rainfall at village level, but most villages have no gauge. I estimate daily rainfall where there's no station, and test it the hard way: whole stations held out, 1° spatial-block folds, and a model frozen before two one-shot locked tests.

The test bed is the ~3,300 Indian rain gauges in GHCN-Daily that were active from 1955 to 1970. That's dense enough to hide 95% of them and score against the truth. ERA5 is the fair gridded baseline for that era, because gauge-based products ingest the test gauges.

  • Gauge-only ML (trained on 438k US and Australia examples, India unseen) ties kriging. The missing information isn't in the neighbouring gauges.
  • The gain comes from new information: ERA5 rain and weather fields (CAPE, column water vapour, moisture divergence, winds), lags, patches and terrain priors, with training geometry matched to deployment density. A leak check on the priors passed.
  • Satellite era: IMERG beats gauge-only interpolation by 17–23%. On near-real-time IMERG Late, the learned merge beats raw satellite by 1–5%, and 5% of gauges come within ~2% of kriging with every gauge.
Open problem. Every method catches at most about a third of days with 64.5 mm or more of rain, and all of them under-predict those days. Those are the insurance and flood triggers, so the next target is heavy-rain detection at a fixed false-alarm rate, not RMSE.

P/02 Reward AtlasA map of RL environments where a program, not a judge model, grades the answer. 380 candidate environments178 with no prior work found 2026
review

A review of reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). It asks which environments haven't been built yet, and whether any of them could become a company.

With a team of research agents, I mapped 54 environment ecosystems, 828 named tasks from the major gyms and 213 open-source verifiers and simulators, flagging the ones never used as an LLM reward. Then adversarial novelty checks went through every candidate.

The result is a catalog of 380 environments in 27 clusters, from PVT-robust analog circuit sizing checked by ngspice to formal assertions that must kill injected RTL bugs. Each entry gives the task, the programmatic verifier, difficulty knobs, the closest prior work, and a startup tier.

Two threads stood out: verified finance-ops agents, and India's GST e-invoice → e-way bill → input-tax-credit chain.

P/03 FleetA crash-safe merge train for many coding agents working one repo in parallel. 0 invariant violations under chaos1,051–1,878 merges/hour 2026
in daily use

Fleet coordinates many cheap coding agents under a planner: a SQLite ticket board, leases with fencing tokens so a stalled worker can never land stale work, a merge train that gates batches before they reach main, and a hook that refuses direct writes to main.

It replaced an earlier queue that broke its own invariants even on clean runs. A chaos bench proves the replacement: it randomly SIGKILLs workers, agents and the merger, freezes workers past their leases, writes to main behind Fleet's back, and hangs the test gate.

  • Release gate: 10/10 chaos runs with 0 invariant violations, 0 stalls and 0 abandoned work, then a 33-run overnight soak at 4–16 workers, also clean.
  • Speculative gating stacks the next train on the tip a passing gate just built: 1.78× more merges per hour on a gate-bound bench.
  • 64 parallel slots reach 2,294 merges/hour, with the whole fleet in 7 processes and 190 MB.
